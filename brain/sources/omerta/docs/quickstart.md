# Omerta brain quickstart

The brain is an offline AI that lives inside the Omerta AI app. It has a personality, remembers everything you teach it, and answers without any network connection.

## First five minutes
Switch the app to the BRAIN provider (Settings → AI PROVIDER → BRAIN, or tap "go fully OFFLINE" on the start screen). Say hello. Then teach it three things about you: your name ("my name is …"), something you like ("I love …"), and a fact you always forget ("remember that …"). Ask for them back.

## Making it yours
Open the Brain screen (the brain icon in the top bar). Give it a name, a tagline, a greeting, traits, a tone (calm, friendly, playful, serious, sarcastic, mentor or hype), catchphrases and a goodbye line. Save the personality and it speaks that way immediately.

## Backing up and sharing
Export the brain as a .brain file. Keep it in your cloud drive, send it to another phone, or edit it on a PC with Brain Studio. Importing a .brain file installs it and makes it active.
